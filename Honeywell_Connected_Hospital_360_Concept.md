# Honeywell Connected Hospital 360

## Concept title

Honeywell Connected Hospital 360: Patient Journey Command Center

## Executive narrative

Honeywell Connected Hospital 360 expands the wearable smart patch idea into a whole-hospital application that follows the patient from OPD to emergency, IPD, OT/ICU, discharge, and post-discharge care. It combines patient monitoring, hospital operations, workflow intelligence, asset visibility, bed management, discharge coordination, and remote care continuity into one command-center platform.

The central idea is simple: hospitals already have clinical excellence and world-class intervention capability. The platform creates the time, visibility, and coordination needed to bring the right patient to the right care team at the right moment.

## Strategic positioning

This is not only a wearable device. It is a hospital operating layer for:

- Patient flow.
- Risk scoring.
- Early warning.
- Bed and capacity visibility.
- Asset and equipment availability.
- Staff task coordination.
- Discharge readiness.
- Post-discharge remote monitoring.
- Leadership-level operational intelligence.

The solution is designed for high-volume India and APAC hospitals where patient load, staff constraints, bed pressure, and multi-department coordination directly affect outcomes and revenue.

## End-to-end patient journey

### 1. OPD and pre-admission

- Appointment and queue visibility.
- Connected vitals capture: BP, SpO2, temperature, ECG snapshot, heart rate.
- Risk pre-screening for cardiac, respiratory, diabetic, elderly, and post-operative follow-up patients.
- Doctor dashboard showing patient history, previous vitals, risk trend, and recommended next action.
- Fast-track routing for high-risk patients to diagnostics, emergency, or admission desk.

### 2. Emergency and triage

- Rapid triage scoring using vitals, symptoms, age, comorbidity, and history.
- Connected ER vitals station.
- Early warning dashboard for emergency physicians and nurse leads.
- OT/ICU readiness signal when deterioration risk crosses threshold.
- Ambulance-to-hospital handoff for pre-arrival vitals and patient context.

### 3. Admission and IPD

- Patient-bed-device assignment.
- Wearable patch monitoring for ECG, heart rate, respiration, temperature, posture, restlessness, and device health.
- Connected BP and SpO2 capture during nurse rounds.
- Nurse task queue for vitals, alerts, medication reminders, and escalation.
- Patient risk score continuously updated by vitals, trends, missed observations, and care context.

### 4. OT, ICU, and step-down

- OT readiness board for patient, bed, staff, equipment, and clearance status.
- ICU/CCU bed visibility and transfer queue.
- Post-procedure monitoring through wearable patch and connected vitals.
- Step-down readiness scoring based on stability trend and care pathway.
- Escalation when a step-down patient begins showing deterioration signs.

### 5. Diagnostics, pharmacy, and support workflows

- Diagnostics queue visibility.
- Pharmacy readiness and discharge medication status.
- Biomedical equipment availability.
- Patient transport requests.
- Housekeeping and bed cleaning queue.
- Department-level SLA and bottleneck dashboards.

### 6. Discharge readiness

- Discharge command center showing clinical, pharmacy, billing, diagnostics, nursing, transport, and housekeeping readiness.
- Discharge delay reason codes.
- Bed cleaning trigger after discharge confirmation.
- Estimated discharge time.
- Family/patient notification workflow.
- Bed release visibility for admission and emergency teams.

### 7. Post-discharge care

- Remote monitoring through wearable patch, connected BP monitor, SpO2, or home vitals kit.
- Medication reminders and adherence tracking.
- Follow-up appointment coordination.
- Readmission risk prediction.
- Escalation to hospital care team when vitals or symptoms cross risk thresholds.
- Patient engagement through mobile app, SMS, WhatsApp, or call-center workflow depending on hospital context.

## Core application modules

### Patient Journey Command Center

Tracks every patient across OPD, emergency, admission, ward, OT/ICU, discharge, and post-discharge care.

### Clinical Risk Engine

Combines vitals, ECG, BP, SpO2, respiration, temperature, symptoms, history, missed observations, and care context into risk scores and escalation queues.

### Bed and Capacity Command Center

Tracks bed status across vacant, occupied, cleaning, blocked, maintenance, discharge-ready, transfer-in-progress, and reserved states.

### Asset Intelligence

Tracks pumps, monitors, wheelchairs, oxygen cylinders, ECG carts, stretchers, defibrillators, portable ultrasound devices, and other mobile assets.

### Discharge Command Center

Coordinates clinical clearance, diagnostics, pharmacy, billing, nursing, transport, housekeeping, and bed release.

### Post-Discharge Remote Care

Extends hospital care beyond discharge through remote monitoring, risk alerts, follow-up coordination, and readmission prevention.

### Leadership Intelligence

Provides real-time and historical KPIs for OPD load, ER wait time, bed occupancy, ICU availability, OT utilization, discharge delays, asset availability, patient risk, readmission risk, and operational bottlenecks.

## Data and integration foundation

- Patient registration, appointment, and queue data.
- ADT events for admission, discharge, and transfer.
- HMS/EHR/EMR integration through FHIR, HL7, REST APIs, or hospital-specific adapters.
- Device telemetry from wearable patch, BP monitor, SpO2, bed sensors, and asset tags.
- Workflow state events from nursing, pharmacy, diagnostics, billing, housekeeping, transport, and biomedical teams.
- Command-center analytics and department-level dashboards.

## Why this matters

Leading hospitals can save lives when the patient reaches the right intervention team in time. Connected Hospital 360 helps create that time window by continuously monitoring patient risk, predicting deterioration earlier, coordinating hospital resources, and ensuring that beds, staff, equipment, and support workflows move together.

- Faster triage and escalation.
- Improved bed utilization and turnover.
- Reduced discharge delays.
- Lower asset search time.
- Better nurse productivity.
- Stronger patient engagement after discharge.
